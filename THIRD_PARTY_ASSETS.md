# Third-party visual assets

Only freely redistributable CC0 assets are pulled by `FreeAssetBootstrap`.
Downloaded files are intentionally ignored by Git and are fetched during Editor/Cloud preparation.

## Policy
Do not add ripped models, branded game vehicles, copyrighted audio, or assets with unclear redistribution rights to this repository. New assets must have their source and license added here before use.
